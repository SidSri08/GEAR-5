# 🏴‍☠️ GEAR 5 — Your Productivity Adventure

> Turn your goals into missions. Build habits. Earn rewards. Become the Pirate King of Productivity!

GEAR 5 is an anime-inspired, gamified productivity companion designed to help students overcome procrastination, build consistency, and manage their daily goals.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 **AI Mission Planner:** Uses Google's Gemma 4 model through Ollama to break large goals into smaller, actionable tasks.
- 🎯 **Task Management:** Organise your goals into manageable missions.
- ⚡ **XP & Level System:** Earn experience points by completing tasks.
- 🪙 **Berries:** Collect virtual rewards as you make progress.
- 🔥 **Progress Tracking:** Monitor your productivity journey.
- 💾 **Local Storage:** Your progress is saved in your browser.

## 🧠 The Problem

Students often struggle to turn large academic or personal goals into smaller, achievable actions. This can lead to procrastination and inconsistent routines.

## 💡 Our Solution

GEAR 5 combines gamification with AI-powered task planning. Users enter a goal, and Gemma 4 suggests smaller missions with estimated completion times.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Node.js
- Express.js
- Ollama
- Gemma 4

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder in your terminal.
3. Install dependencies:

   `npm install`

4. Make sure Ollama is installed and Gemma 4 is downloaded:

   `ollama pull gemma4:e2b`

5. Start the application:

   `node server.js`

6. Open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.
